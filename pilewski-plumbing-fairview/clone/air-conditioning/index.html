Air Conditioning

Beat the heat this summer with air conditioning services from Pilewski Plumbing, Inc. in Fairview, PA. We can install energy-efficient air conditioning systems that will provide fresh, cool air when it’s hot and humid outside. As with your heating system, we recommend assessing your air conditioning system if your system is more than 10 years old, as you could be spending more on your energy bills than you should.

Ductless Air Conditioning

Ductless Air Conditioning is becoming more common because of its energy efficiency properties and because you can target individual rooms. Traditional units can lose up to 30% efficiency through ductwork. Want to cool the upstairs bedrooms during those hot nights, but keep the downstairs at a manageable temperature? This is the system for you. We install and service Mitsubishi ductless systems.

Dehumidifiers

Another way to cool your house down without completely relying on an air conditioning unit is a dehumidifier. These help eliminate moisture in the air and make you feel more comfortable. As a bonus, they also improve air quality, prevent mold, and help eliminate dust mites.

Air Conditioning FAQs

What air conditioning services do you provide?

We offer installation, repair, and maintenance services for various air conditioning systems to keep your home cool during the warmer months.

How can I improve the efficiency of my cooling system?

Regular maintenance, such as cleaning filters and checking for refrigerant leaks, can enhance efficiency. Our team can perform comprehensive tune-ups to optimize your system’s performance.

Do you install air conditioning systems in new constructions?

Yes, we provide installation services for new construction projects, ensuring your cooling system is properly integrated and meets your comfort needs.

What should I do if my air conditioner isn't cooling properly?

If your AC isn’t cooling effectively, it could be due to issues like refrigerant leaks, dirty filters, or thermostat problems. We can diagnose the issue and perform the necessary repairs.

Can you help with upgrading to a more energy-efficient cooling system?

Absolutely. We can assess your current system and recommend energy-efficient options that can reduce your energy bills and improve comfort.
