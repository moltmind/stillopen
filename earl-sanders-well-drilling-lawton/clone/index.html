Southwest Michigan Well Drilling and Water Services

For over 70 years and three generations (since 1943!), Earl Sanders & Son water well drilling has been proud to serve our customers’ water well system needs in Kalamazoo, Lawton, Paw Paw and surrounding areas. From well drilling for residential and irrigation purposes to pump service and well rehabilitation, we have always been there for our customers. This tradition of quality and workmanship is what sets us apart.

You can depend on us for superior well services. Your work will be done by fully-trained staff who can make sure you get the results that you need in a timely, affordable manner. Your complete satisfaction is our top priority.
