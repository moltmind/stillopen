Directional Drilling & HDD Drilling Services

Some sites can't be reached with a conventional vertical rig. Obstacles—utilities, structures, rock formations, and environmental setbacks—require a different approach.

Directional drilling and horizontal directional drilling (HDD) allow our crews to reach water sources that would otherwise be inaccessible without surface disruption. HDD drilling is particularly valuable for installations near buildings, under roadways, or in areas where minimal site disturbance is a priority.

Our directional drilling crew is one of the most experienced in the region. Adam oversees operations; DJ runs the rig. Between them, they've handled some of the most technically demanding installations in southeast Wisconsin.

Well Inspection Services & Water Well Maintenance

Buying a property with an existing well? Lenders, insurers, and buyers increasingly require a formal well inspection before closing. Skipping it is one of the costlier mistakes a buyer can make.

Our well inspection services cover casing integrity, pump condition, water quality sampling, flow rate testing, and pressure system evaluation. We document findings clearly—so buyers, sellers, and agents all walk away with accurate information.

Beyond inspections, water well maintenance is the kind of thing that gets deferred until there's a problem. Routine checks on pump performance, pressure, and water quality catch issues early, before they become expensive.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What areas does Herr Well Drilling serve?

     We're based in Dousman, WI and serve Metro Milwaukee and Southeast Wisconsin, including Waukesha, Oconomowoc, Pewaukee, Hartland, Wales, and surrounding communities. We also cover portions of Dane County and properties within about 50 miles of Dousman.

  • How long has Herr Well Drilling been in business?

    Since 1964. We're now in our fifth generation of licensed well drillers, all operating in Southeast Wisconsin. It's the only market we've ever worked in.

  • Do you drill wells year-round?

     Yes. Our equipment is built to operate in Wisconsin winter conditions. Ground freeze doesn't stop us.

  • What types of wells do you drill?

    Residential, commercial, and agricultural wells—including deep wells, high-capacity wells, and irrigation wells. We also handle directional and horizontal directional drilling for sites where conventional vertical drilling isn't an option.

  • How much does a new well cost?

    It depends on depth, well type, site conditions, and what pump and pressure system the property requires. We don't quote flat rates because flat rates aren't honest in this business. Contact us for a free, site-specific estimate.

  • How long does well drilling take?

     Most residential wells are completed in one to two days. Agricultural and commercial wells vary based on depth and complexity. We give you a realistic timeline upfront.

  • Can you repair a well drilled by another contractor?

     Yes. We service and repair all types and brands of well systems regardless of who originally installed them.

  • How do I know if I need a new well or just a repair?

     That's exactly the kind of question a site evaluation answers. In many cases, what looks like a failing well is actually a pump or pressure system issue—which is significantly less expensive to fix. We diagnose honestly and give you a straight answer before recommending any work.
