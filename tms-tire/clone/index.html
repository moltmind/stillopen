Thank you for checking out our website. Likely, you’re here for one of three reasons:

Your tires are wearing out, are too loud, or they just don’t feel right.
It's time to get your oil changed, or fix whatever turned on that little check engine light.
You’re looking for a friendly, honest auto mechanic to answer your questions and get rid of those weird noises or smells your car is making.
At TMS Tire & Auto Complete Automotive Care we have the experience to help you with all your auto repair and tire needs.

Why should you choose TMS Tire & Auto Complete Automotive Care?

Every business claims to be the best. At TMS Tire & Auto Complete Automotive Care, we’d rather let our work and loyal customers speak for us. Check out our promotions page for limited-time offers on tires and auto repairs. If you’re still not convinced that we provide the tires and auto repair services you’re looking for, then call us or come see us, and see how our friendly staff will go above and beyond to help you with your auto repair needs.

Our mission here at TMS Tire & Auto Complete Automotive Care is to perform fast efficient service at a fair and competitive price.
