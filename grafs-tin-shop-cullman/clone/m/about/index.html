About

Graf's Tin Shop

Our Story

Locally owned and operated in Cullman, AL, Graf's Tin Shop was founded by Mr. Graf in 1926 and offers HVAC repair, installation, and maintenance, as well as custom metal work. The business was purchased by its current owner April Ray's grandfather, Alvin Schmidt, in the 1970s. When her grandfather retired, April's parents took over the business and when her parents retired, it was passed on to her. Graf's Tin Shop is now run by April and her husband, Jon Ray. The majority of the clients have worked with the business since back when April's grandfather was the owner.


Contact us today for any of our HVAC services or to request our custom metalwork. You'll always hear back the same day.
