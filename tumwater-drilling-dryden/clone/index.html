KEEP IN TOUCH
Why should You use Tumwater Drilling and Pump Inc? Because we are a full service water well resource. Since 1974, we have been providing the highest standard of Washington well drilling services to Chelan, Douglas, Kittitas, and Grant Counties. With well over 100 years of combined well experience, our staff will help you accomplish your goals. Whether you need a new water well in Ellensburg or surrounding areas, need pump service, need to test your water for bacteria, iron, sulfur or hardness, or simply need an irrigation part our staff can help. We use quality products with the goal that they will give you years of service. Most importantly, we carry all of the necessary licenses and certifications for drilling water wells, installing pumps and performing water treatment in Washington State so be assured we are here to serve you in a reliable and professional manner. 

Water Well Drilling
Specialize in 6"-10" domestic and irrigation wells. We can do a complimentary water viability assessment, meet on-site, and provide expert advice and service. 

Pump Sales and Service
Submersible Pumps, Above Ground Pumps, Solar Pumps, Hand Pumps, Pressure Tanks, Switches and Control Boxes and Constant Pressure Conversion

Water Treatment, Conditioning, Softening, Purification, UV and Filtration 
Our water treatment specialist can test your water in-house or on-site and provide a solution to treat your troubled well water.

Maintenance
Ongoing maintenance serves as prevention to keep minor problems from turning into major repairs. We are available for system maintenance, helping to minimize system failures.

Repairs
We stock a full line of submersible pumps and motors and can have repairs completed in a timely manner. 

Retail Shop
Our Retail Shop offers a selection of irrigation supplies, sewer pipe and fittings, electrical conduit and fittings, replacement filters, salt, pex and brass fittings.

Flow Tests
Whether you have a pump or not, we can test your well flow production and provide an accurate report for your Health Department, personal information, real estate transactions, and Group B Designs. We have a large selection of test pumps.

System Checks
We can give your water system an assessment and provide a detailed report for preventative maintenance or real estate transactions.
