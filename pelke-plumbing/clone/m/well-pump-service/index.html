Well Pump Services When You Need It Most

Your well pump delivers fresh water to your home or business. When it stops working, you need fast repairs you can count on. Pelke Plumbing & Well Drilling Inc provides complete well pump service throughout West Central Wisconsin and the surrounding area. Our services include:

  • Well pump repairs and replacements
  • Pressure tank inspections
  • System diagnostics and troubleshooting
  • Preventive maintenance programs
  • Emergency pump services
  • Performance optimization


Reach out to us today for prompt well pump service.


Signs You Need Well Pump Service

Spotting the warning signs of well pump problems can save you from costly water damage and system failure. Here's what to watch for:

  • Weak or inconsistent water pressure
  • Sputtering faucets or air in the lines
  • Unusual noises from the pump
  • Constantly running pump
  • Cloudy or discolored water
  • Higher than normal electric bills


Act quickly when you notice these signs. These issues typically get worse over time, leading to complete pump failure and potential water system damage.
