The Journey of Excellence – Meet Brian Campbell

Our story began with Brian Campbell following in his father’s footsteps into the HVAC industry. Starting work during summers and steadily developing his expertise, Brian earned his own HVAC license in 2002. Shortly after, he moved to Flagstaff, Arizona, where he established Campbell Heating & Cooling—a family-centered business that has flourished in the local community.

Brian’s family shares his love of Flagstaff, and the strong connection between family and business continues to be a hallmark of Campbell’s A/C LLC. With a passion for excellence, Brian envisions future generations carrying forward the legacy of quality service and innovation in the HVAC industry.
