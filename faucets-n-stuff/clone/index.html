Faucets ‘n Stuff Plumbing

Faucets ‘n Stuff Plumbing has been serving the greater Spokane Washington area for fifty years. 100% owner operated, we pride ourselves on going above and beyond for our customers.

We start by listening to your needs and then offering you the best, most cost-effective solution to your plumbing problem. Faucets ‘n Stuff Plumbing will explain what needs to be done to complete your job correctly and tell you all costs upfront. When we work on your home or business, you can be assured that the job will be done right the first time. You can expect an enjoyable, hassle free experience and trust that we will take care of your plumbing needs in Spokane Washington.

We are ready to fix your plumbing issue 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
