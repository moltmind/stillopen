Hearth

Keeping your home comfortable.

  • Gas

    We offer a wide range of heating solutions, including gas stoves, fireplaces, inserts, and log sets. Our gas stoves provide efficient warmth and can enhance the aesthetic of any room. Fireplaces add a cozy ambiance to your home, while gas inserts are designed to provide an updated, efficient option for existing fireplaces. Additionally, our log sets can easily transform your fireplace into a beautiful and easy-to-use focal point. We prioritize quality and energy efficiency in all of our products to ensure effective heating and customer satisfaction.

  • Wood

    With a comprehensive selection of wood stoves, fireplaces, and inserts designed to enhance any living space while providing efficient heating solutions. Our wood stoves deliver traditional charm and functionality, making them ideal for those who appreciate a classic aesthetic coupled with modern efficiency. Fireplaces can serve as a stunning focal point in your home, available in various styles to suit diverse tastes and preferences. Additionally, our inserts provide a seamless way to transform existing fireplaces into more energy-efficient heating options, maximizing warmth while minimizing environmental impact. Each product is chosen for its quality and performance, ensuring that you can enjoy a comfortable and inviting atmosphere in your home year-round.

  • Pellet

    Burning pellets provides several benefits as an efficient, eco-friendly fuel. Made from compressed organic materials, they produce fewer carbon emissions than fossil fuels. Their uniform size ensures consistent burns and high heat output, enhancing heating efficiency. Often sourced from renewables, pellets reduce reliance on non-renewable energy. They can lower heating costs due to high energy density and affordability compared to oil or natural gas. Additionally, pellet stoves offer convenient loading and automated feeding, making them appealing for residential heating.

  • Electric

    We provide a diverse selection of electric fireplaces designed to enhance the ambiance of any space. Our electric fireplaces provide a realistic flame effect, adjustable heat settings, and customizable options to fit your style and comfort needs. Whether you're looking for a traditional, modern, or contemporary design, we have the perfect solution to add warmth and charm to your home without the need for a traditional chimney or venting. Enjoy the benefits of easy installation, safety features, and energy efficiency with our electric fireplaces. Upgrade your living space with the elegance and convenience of our electric fireplace offerings.
