Terms of Service

Last updated: November 3, 2026

These terms are between your shop and StillOpen. StillOpen is run by me, Cole Cummings, in Menomonie, Wisconsin. StillOpen is operated by Cole Cummings, a sole proprietor doing business as StillOpen.

When I say "you" or "the shop," I mean the business that signs up. When I say "callers," I mean the people who call your shop.

1. What StillOpen is

StillOpen is an after-hours phone desk for your shop.

  • You keep your phone number.
  • When nobody picks up after four rings, the call rolls to the desk.
  • The desk answers as your shop, in English or Spanish.
  • It takes the caller's name, phone number and address.
  • It tells the caller they are first in line for your next opening and that the owner will confirm the time.
  • It does not give arrival windows.
  • You get each caller's details by email, and every call is on your morning report page.

If the caller says they smell gas, the desk gives them a safety line first. Then it offers to transfer them to your on-call phone.

The desk never tells a caller a time. You set every time with the customer.

2. What StillOpen is not

  • Not an emergency service. The desk is not 9-1-1. Any caller who says they are in danger is told to hang up and call 9-1-1.
  • Not a dispatcher. The desk does not send a truck, assign a tech or set an arrival time. You do that.
  • Not a person. The desk is an automated voice system. It can mishear things, miss things or get cut off.
  • Not a text service. The desk does not send text messages to you or to your callers.
  • Not a payment taker. The desk does not take payments or deposits over the phone.
  • Not a quote line. The desk only says what you tell me it can say about your services. It does not quote prices you have not approved.

3. Your part

To make this work, you agree to:

  • Give me accurate info. Your business name, hours, services, service area, and what the desk should never quote. Tell me when any of it changes.
  • Keep a working on-call number. Gas-smell calls get offered a transfer to that number. Someone needs to answer it. If it changes, tell me right away.
  • Set up call forwarding. You (or your phone company) set your line to forward unanswered calls after four rings to the number I give you.
  • Follow up with callers. The desk tells callers you will confirm the time. You are the one who calls them back.
  • Tell customers where the law requires it. If a law where you do business says customers must be told an automated service answers after hours, you are responsible for telling them. That could be a line on your website, your invoices or your voicemail.
  • Use it legally. Don't use the desk for anything illegal or to mislead callers.

4. Price and billing

  • $497 to go live. One time. This covers the setup: building your desk, the phone number, testing and go-live.
  • $197 a month after that.
  • Payments run through Stripe. When you sign up, you pay the $497 and your first month's $197 together, $694 that day. After that, the $197 runs on that same date each month.
  • If a payment fails, I'll email you. If it is still unpaid 14 days later, I may pause the desk until it is paid.
  • Prices do not include any charges your phone company makes for call forwarding.

5. The 30-day guarantee

If the desk has not caught your shop a job within 30 days of going live, I refund the $497 in full.

"Caught a job" means at least one after-hours caller asked for a service your shop offers, inside your service area, and the desk got their name, phone number and address and sent them to you.

To use the guarantee, email cole@stillopen.ai or call (920) 332-6113 within 7 days after the 30 days end. The guarantee covers the $497 go-live fee. It does not cover monthly fees already paid.

6. Canceling

You can cancel any time. No contract term, no cancellation fee.

  • Email cole@stillopen.ai, or
  • Call (920) 332-6113.

The desk keeps running until the end of the month you already paid for. I don't refund partial months.

When you cancel, turn off call forwarding on your phone line. I'll send you the steps. The phone number I assigned to your desk goes back to me.

I can also end service with 30 days' notice by email. If I do, I'll refund any month you paid for and didn't get. I can end service right away if the desk is being used illegally or to harm callers.

7. What I store and for how long

To run the desk and show you your calls, StillOpen stores:

  • Call details: who called, their number, the time, how long the call was, and what they said they needed.
  • Transcripts: a written version of each call.
  • Your shop's setup: hours, services, service area, on-call number, and the rules you gave me.

I keep call details and transcripts for 90 days, then they are deleted. When you cancel, I delete your shop's call records within 30 days, unless you ask me to send you a copy first. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

The full list of where data goes, and who sees it, is in the Privacy Policy.

8. Call audio and transcripts

The desk does not keep a recording of the call audio. While the call is going, the caller's words are turned into text so the desk can answer, and you get the written transcript afterward.

Wisconsin is a one-party consent state. That means a call can be recorded or transcribed when one person on the call agrees to it. Your shop is the party here.

If your shop takes calls from, or does business in, a state that requires more notice, you are responsible for giving that notice. If you want, the desk can open every call with "This call may be transcribed." Tell me at setup.

9. Limits on what I owe you

I'll do my best to keep the desk running and accurate. But I can't promise it will never fail. Phone companies go down. Internet goes down. The desk can mishear a name or an address.

In plain words:

  • The service comes as it is. I don't promise it will catch every call or get every detail right.
  • I'm not responsible for lost jobs, lost profits, or what happens on a job. The desk takes a message. What happens after that is between you and your customer.
  • I'm not responsible for emergencies. The desk tells people in danger to call 9-1-1 and offers gas-smell callers your on-call phone. It cannot do more than that.
  • The most I'll ever owe you for anything related to StillOpen is what you paid me in the 3 months before the problem.

If a caller makes a claim against me because of something your shop did or told me to say, you agree to cover that cost.

Some states don't allow some of these limits. Where that's true, the limit applies only as far as the law allows.
