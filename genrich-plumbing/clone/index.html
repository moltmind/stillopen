Serving Wausau For Over 50 Years

With a shared excitement to build a solid foundation, Ed and Pat Genrich incorporated Genrich Plumbing Co. in 1964, launching their new plumbing business in the Wausau area. Starting out as just a two-person operation – Ed as the Master Plumber and Pat as Secretary – the company steadily grew thanks to a focus on plumbing, quality workmanship, and excellent customer service.

Over time, Genrich Plumbing established itself as a leading provider of plumbing, known for skilled craftsmanship and outstanding service throughout the Wausau community.

Today, we’re led by Master Plumber Bob Genrich, handling a wide range of residential plumbing needs including fixture installations and replacements, complete bathroom remodels, leak repairs, and more. We follow all codes and regulations to ensure our work is up to the highest standards.
