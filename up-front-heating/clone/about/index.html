About Us

Up-Front Heating & Air LLC is a Baldwin, Wisconsin-based, family-owned HVAC company specializing in residential forced-air system repairs, installations, and maintenance. Riley Nowicki, owner / operator, is an experienced, qualified, and certified HVAC technician.

Honesty. Integrity. Professionalism

At Up-Front, we take pride in our “no-nonsense” approach. When our team walks into your home, you can expect a friendly face, a clean workspace, and an honest explanation of what’s going on with your system. 

Our Mission

At Up-front Heating & Air, we aren’t just another HVAC company; we’re your neighbors. Our team is comprised of highly trained, experienced, and certified professionals.

Unlike a lot of companies out there, we are fully transparent when diagnosing issues with your system. We will walk you through any problems found and provide you with options.

Core Values

  • We consider honesty as the key to our business's integrity.
  • We always do the right thing to solve our customer’s problem.
  • We perform all work to the highest standards
  • We treat your home as if it were ours

Why Choose Us?

  • Fully licensed, bonded, & insured

  • 100% satisfaction guaranteed
