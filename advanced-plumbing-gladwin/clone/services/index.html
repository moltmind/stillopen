Take the Taste Out of Your Water

Spending too much time and money scrubbing rust?

We provide water treatment solutions to those who are dealing with everyday issues associated with well water by installing Oxy Blast® water treatment systems and also carry product refills. It gives you clean nutritious water for pennies a day. The core of Oxy Blast is composed of two important elements, hydrogen and oxygen. When Oxy Blast combines with water, it increases the oxygen content in the water, yielding clearer, cleaner water. 


Applications

  • Clearing water contaminants
  • Increased water line pressure
  • Providing better tasting water in homes
  • Improving water consumption rates in livestock and poultry operations
  • Increasing water clarity in ponds and fountain features

Video Sewer Inspection

In years past, dig points were a matter of educated guesswork. A few exploratory holes might be augured, with more misses than hits. Today, a relatively low-cost method is the better way to do this: a sewer camera inspection. A camera is pushed through the line to locate the exact location of the blockage.

Oxy Blast® Water Treatment System

Oxy Blast is a water treatment that has been used successfully in the well water treatment and animal production industries. It is composed of hydrogen and oxygen, and when it combines with water, it increases the oxygen content in the water, yielding clearer, cleaner water. Oxy Blast can clean up water contaminants, well pumps, water lines, waterers, stock tanks, and holding tanks. Producers report better overall health of livestock and poultry that leads to better production.

ASSE Certified Back-Flow Testing

ASSE backflow certification is a professional certification in the specialized segment of backflow prevention. It allows the holder, who must also hold a current plumbing license, to perform the installation, maintenance, testing, repair, replacement or rebuilding of backflow prevention assemblies.
