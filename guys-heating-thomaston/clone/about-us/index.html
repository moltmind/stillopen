About us
Guy's Heating & Cooling LLC is based on the belief that our customers' needs are of the utmost importance. Our entire team is committed to meeting those needs. As a result, the majority of our business is generated from repeat customers and referrals.

We would welcome the opportunity to earn your trust and deliver to you the best service and installation in the industry.

 

Our history began in the 1930's, with Guy's grandfather opening a plumbing and heating business in Fairfield County. Since then, we have evolved into the fourth generation of family-owned and operated business, with Guy's Heating & Cooling LLC, now serving all of Fairfield, New Haven, and Litchfield Counties. Consider us your "Hometown Heating and Cooling Guys".
