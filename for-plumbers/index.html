Straight answers

The questions plumbers actually ask.

Does it quote repair prices?

No, and that's on purpose. A good plumber doesn't price a fix he hasn't seen. It quotes your published diagnostic or service-call fee, explains that it goes toward the job, and books the visit.

What if it doesn't know something?

It says so and takes a message. It never invents a price, a service, or a service area. If it isn't on your site, it isn't in its mouth.

Can it take a deposit for a midnight call?

Yes, through your own Stripe. That's how you stop driving out at 1 AM for somebody who changed their mind.
