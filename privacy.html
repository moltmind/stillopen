Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

StillOpen is an after-hours phone desk for plumbing and heating shops. It is run by me, Cole Cummings, in Menomonie, Wisconsin. StillOpen is operated by Cole Cummings, a sole proprietor doing business as StillOpen.

This policy covers three groups of people:

  1. Shop owners who sign up for StillOpen.
  2. Callers who phone a shop and reach the desk after hours.
  3. Facebook users who message a shop's Facebook Page, if the shop connected its Page to StillOpen.

The short version: I collect what the desk needs to take a message and get it to the shop. I don't sell anyone's data. You can ask me to delete it.

Part 1: Shop owners

What I collect from you

  • Your name, business name, email and phone numbers, including your on-call number.
  • Your hours, services, service area, and the things the desk should never quote.
  • Your shop's public website, which I read to build the desk's info.
  • Billing info. Stripe handles your card. I see things like the last four digits and whether a payment went through. I never see or store your full card number.

Why

To set up your desk, answer calls the way you want, send you call details, show you your morning report page, and bill you.

How long I keep it

As long as you're a customer. After you cancel, I delete your setup info within 30 days. I keep billing records as long as tax law requires.

Part 2: Callers

If you called a shop after hours and reached the desk, this part is for you.

What I collect from you

  • Your phone number, and the time and length of your call.
  • Your name and address, if you give them.
  • What you said you needed.
  • A transcript: a written version of the call.

The desk does not keep a recording of the call audio. Your words are turned into text during the call so the desk can answer you.

Why

To pass your message to the shop you called, so they can call you back and set up a time. The shop sees your details. I use them to run the desk and fix problems with it.

The desk does not take payments, does not send you text messages, and does not promise you a time. The shop confirms the time with you.

How long I keep it

90 days, then it is deleted. The shop you called may keep its own copy of your details from the emails I send them. That copy is under the shop's control, not mine.

Part 3: Facebook Messenger

Some shops connect their Facebook Page so the desk can answer Messenger messages too. When a shop does:

  • From the shop: the Page's ID and name, and a Page access token from Meta. I use the token only to receive messages sent to that Page and to reply on the shop's behalf.
  • From people who message the Page: the Page-scoped ID Meta gives you for that Page (it isn't your Facebook user ID), what you write, and any name, number or address you share.
  • Meta delivers the replies, so it gets the reply text and your Page-scoped ID.

Messages in your own Messenger inbox are under Meta's control. To delete what StillOpen has, use the data deletion page.

Where the data is stored

StillOpen uses a few companies to run the desk. Your data passes through or is stored with them:

CompanyWhat it does for StillOpen
TwilioPhone calls: the phone number and connecting the call.
DeepgramTurns the caller's speech into text during the call. Runs through Twilio.
ElevenLabsThe desk's voice. Runs through Twilio.
AnthropicThe AI model that understands the caller and writes the summary.
CloudflareHosting and storage. Call details and transcripts are stored here.
ResendSends the emails to shop owners with call details.
ClerkSign-in for the shop owner's morning report page.
StripePayments from shop owners. Callers never pay through StillOpen.
FirecrawlReads the shop's public website when the desk is set up. No caller data.
MetaDelivers Messenger replies, only for shops that connect a Facebook Page.

Each of these companies has its own privacy policy. I only share what each one needs to do its job.

Who sees it

  • Shop owners see their own shop's calls. Never another shop's.
  • I (Cole) can see everything, because I run and fix the service.
  • Nobody else, except the companies listed above as they handle it, or if the law requires me to hand it over.

I don't sell data

I don't sell, rent or trade anyone's data. Not shop owners'. Not callers'. I don't use it for ads.

Deleting your data

You can ask me to delete your data at any time.

  • Use the data deletion page
  • Or email cole@stillopen.ai
  • Or call (920) 332-6113

Tell me the phone number you called from (callers) or your business name (shop owners). I'll delete it from StillOpen within 30 days and confirm by email or phone. If you're a caller, I'll also let the shop know you asked, since they may have their own copy.

Security

I keep access to the data locked down to my own accounts and the companies listed above. No system is perfect. If I learn your data was exposed, I'll tell you.

Kids

StillOpen is for businesses. It isn't meant for children. If a child calls a shop, the desk treats it like any other call. A parent can ask me to delete it.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top. If the change is big, I'll email shop owners first.
