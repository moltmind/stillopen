PROFESSIONAL PLUMBING AND HVAC SERVICES

When you need a reliable plumber or HVAC technician in the Hot Springs area, look no further than City Plumbing Heating & Electric, Inc. With over 30 years of service, our award-winning professionals are dedicated to providing top-quality plumbing, heating, and cooling solutions with efficiency and integrity. From clogged drains and AC repairs to water heater service and full system installations, we’re ready to resolve any issues that may arise in your home.


Expert Plumbing Services

At City Plumbing Heating & Electric, Inc., our plumbers are highly skilled in diagnosing and repairing a wide range of plumbing issues. From gas and electric water heater services to slab leaks, leaky pipes, fixture installations, and backflow prevention, we handle it all. We also offer professional drain cleaning and the installation of new plumbing systems. Every project is completed with a combination of technical expertise and exceptional customer service to ensure your complete satisfaction.

Comprehensive HVAC Solutions

Our HVAC technicians are experts in heating and air conditioning services, ensuring your home stays comfortable all year long. Whether your system isn’t cooling or heating properly, or you need a complete AC installation or replacement, we have the knowledge and experience to get the job done right. We work with condensers, coils, air ducts, and air filtration systems, and we offer some of the best warranties in Hot Springs. Ask about our HVAC Maintenance Agreement—annual tune-ups can prevent many common issues and keep your system running at peak performance.


Dedicated to Excellence

At City Plumbing Heating & Electric, Inc., we take pride in delivering prompt, professional service that exceeds expectations. Whether you call for plumbing or HVAC work, you can expect quality craftsmanship, clear communication, and dependable results every time.
