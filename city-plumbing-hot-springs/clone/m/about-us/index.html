About City Plumbing Heating & Electric, Inc.

A Legacy of Excellence in Hot Springs

City Plumbing Heating & Electric, Inc. has been proudly serving the Hot Springs community since its establishment in 1990. Alongside Jim Fryar's Air Conditioning, Heating & Refrigeration, founded in 1980, we extend our services across Garland, Montgomery, Pike, Hot Spring, and Saline Counties. Both companies have always been locally owned and operated, enjoying rapid growth and success based on referrals and a commitment to superior workmanship.

Our Commitment to Superior Service

We are dedicated to providing service that exceeds expectations. All our technicians are fully trained, drug-free, and undergo thorough criminal background checks. Your safety, and that of your family and home, is our top priority. Our trucks are stocked with a wide selection of repair items, tools, and emergency equipment, enabling us to deliver excellent service promptly while at your home.


Professionalism You Can Trust

  • Transparent Pricing: Our technicians explain the service and provide the fee in advance, so you know exactly what to expect.
  • Neat Appearance: Our technicians dress neatly in uniform, tuck in their shirts, and wear shoe covers inside your home or business. They park in the street to avoid blocking your driveway and walk respectfully to your door.
  • Pride in Presentation: Our office, trucks, equipment, and personal appearance reflect our pride and professionalism.
