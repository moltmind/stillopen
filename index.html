Does my number change?

No. Your phone rings like always. If nobody picks up by the fourth ring, the call rolls over to the desk. You can turn the forwarding off whenever you want.

Is it a person?

No, and it won't pretend to be. If a caller asks, it says, “No, you've got [your shop]'s after-hours answering service. It's automated, not a person.” It answers as your shop and never says StillOpen to your customer.

What does it do if somebody smells gas?

Before anything else, it says: “Get everyone out of the house right now. Don't flip any switches or use anything electric on the way out. Once you're outside, call the gas company or nine one one.” That line is built in, so it goes first every time. Then it offers to connect them to your on-call phone, and it only transfers on a yes.

Does it promise the customer a time?

No. It takes the name, number and address, tells them they're first in line for the next opening, and says you'll call to confirm the time. It never gives an arrival window. You know where your trucks are. It doesn't.
