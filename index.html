Straight answers

What it does and what it doesn't.

It doesn't guess.

It won't quote a repair it hasn't seen, and it won't send anybody out on its own. It quotes your published service-call fee, takes the name, number, and address, books the visit, and for a true emergency it can ring your on-call phone straight through. If it doesn't know something, it says so and takes a message. The phone line is new as of October 2026. Call it before you buy it.

Does my number change?

No. Your phone rings like always. Only the calls nobody picks up roll to the desk. You can turn the forwarding off from your phone any time.

What if it books something wrong?

It only uses what you told it: your hours, services, area, and fees. It books two-hour windows into your Google Calendar and never over an existing job. If a caller wants something it can't confirm, it takes a message instead of promising.

Half my calls are price shoppers and robocalls.

Good, that's why it answers them instead of you. Shoppers get your service-call fee and an offer to book. Robocalls get nothing. You only get a text for a real person with a real job.

Does it sound like a robot?

Call (920) 332-6113 and decide for yourself. If a caller asks plainly whether they're talking to a person, it says it's the front desk the owner set up, and keeps helping.

Can it take a deposit on a midnight call?

Yes, through your own Stripe. It texts the caller a deposit link for the emergency slot, so you stop driving out at 1 AM for somebody who changed their mind.

How long does setup take?

You give us your website, or ten minutes on the phone if you don't have one. We build the desk around your real services, hours, and fees, hand you a number to forward to, and you're live within 48 hours.
