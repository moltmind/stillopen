In-Floor Radiant Heat

Radiant floor heating is one of the most comfortable and energy-efficient heating systems available. Instead of heating the air, radiant systems warm the floor itself, creating even temperatures throughout the room without drafts or cold spots.


Warne Plumbing & Heating designs and installs custom in-floor radiant heating systems for homes, garages, commercial buildings, and agricultural facilities.


Radiant floor heating delivers comfortable warmth exactly where you need it while creating a clean, efficient heating solution for virtually any building.

Schedule Service

Radiant heating is ideal for:

  • New homes
  • Home additions
  • Basements
  • Bathrooms
  • Garages
  • Shops
  • Pole buildings
  • Commercial buildings

Benefits of In-Floor Radiant Heating

  • Even heat throughout the room
  • Exceptional comfort
  • Quiet operation
  • Improved energy efficiency
  • Reduced airborne dust
  • Lower operating costs
  • No visible vents or registers

Radiant Tube Heating

Radiant tube heating is an excellent option for heating large open buildings where traditional forced-air systems may struggle to provide consistent comfort. These systems warm people and objects directly instead of simply heating the air, making them highly efficient in larger spaces.

Radiant tube heaters are commonly installed in:

  • Commercial buildings
  • Warehouses
  • Shops
  • Manufacturing facilities
  • Agricultural buildings
  • Equipment storage buildings
  • Service garages
  • Aircraft hangars

Benefits of Radiant Tube Heating

  • Energy-efficient operation
  • Fast, even heating
  • Excellent performance in large buildings
  • Reduced heat loss
  • Quiet operation
  • Lower operating costs

Radiant tube heating provides dependable warmth even in buildings with high ceilings or frequently opened overhead doors.
