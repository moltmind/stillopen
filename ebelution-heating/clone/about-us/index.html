About Us

Ebelution Heating and Cooling Experts is owned by Joshua & Alicia Ebel. Ebelution Heating and Cooling Experts was established in 2015 because Joshua & Alicia saw a need for quality Heating, Cooling and Maintenance services that people can rely on. Ebelution is a local family owned business serving Rapid City and the Black Hills of South Dakota with 13 years of Residential and Commercial installation, maintenance, and service experience.

Joshua Ebel has been working in the heating & cooling business for over 15 years. After gaining a broad background of experience and realizing the value that he could bring to the industry, he launched Ebelution Heating & Cooling Experts with the primary mission of bringing top quality products to consumers while providing the best value for the money. Ebelution Heating and Cooling Experts prides themselves by handling any job with the utmost professionalism and completion in a timely manner.
