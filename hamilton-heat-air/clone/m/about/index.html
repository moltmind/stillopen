Danny Hamilton

Commit your work to the LORD, and your plans will be established.  - Proverbs 16:3

Family Business

Danny Hamilton is the owner of Hamilton Heat and Air. Danny specializes in installation and service of residential and light commercial heating and air conditioning systems. His wife, Kelly, works alongside him as the business manager.

Hamilton Heat & Air is proud to have been selected by P.S.O. as a 2020 Boundless Energy Partner of the Year!

Danny has multiple years of experience and training in the industry. Danny’s long-term goal has always been to open his own Heating and Air company where he can build his reputation by incorporating his personal values of integrity, dependability, and quality service at affordable prices.

Through diligent work and commitment to customer satisfaction, Danny has established himself as a leader in the industry. He believes that integrity is the most important asset to have both professionally and personally. It means being honest, fair, dependable, and trustworthy. It means taking accountability.

Danny comes from a large family, the oldest of six children. He is the father of six children as well and grandfather of 5. He serves as a mentor and leader at his church and ministry. Diligent work is second nature to Danny. It is what shaped his character.
