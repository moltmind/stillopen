YOUR HOMETOWN PLUMBER

Proudly owned by Edgerton native, Nick Edwardson, Edwardson Plumbing has been providing Edgerton and southern Wisconsin with trustworthy, reliable plumbing services since 2011. Nick has deep roots in our community and treats everyone he encounters like family. You'd be hard pressed to find a friendlier, more trustworthy person!


At Edwardson Plumbing, we believe a good plumber is more than just someone you hire to replace some parts, hand you a bill, and leave a mess behind. We believe a good plumber is courteous and respectful, walks you through each problem and solution and leaves your space the way it was found.

﻿

With quick turnaround times on repairs, replacements, installations and remodels, we will get your plumbing working correctly so you can get back to normal.

Why Choose Edwardson Plumbing?

Edwardson Plumbing's dedicated team of fully insured and certified plumbers provides exceptional plumbing services to the Edgerton, WI community as well as all of southern Wisconsin. With quick turnaround times and same-day emergency service on weekdays, we prioritize your comfort and time. Our one-year workmanship warranty and discounts for seniors and veterans make our services accessible to all. Experience satisfaction and a comfortable home with Edwardson Plumbing LLC.
