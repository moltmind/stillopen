About Us

Nick Edwardson formed Edwardson Plumbing, LLC with the ambition of creating a reliable, reasonable, and honest plumbing service for the local communities. We take pride in our quality workmanship and dedication to our customers. Good service isn’t just about showing up on time or fixing the problem, it’s about your experience with us.


Edwardson Plumbing takes time to assess your situation and get your plumbing repaired for you with as little inconvenience to you as possible. We are fully insured and have a staff of licensed and experienced technicians ready to handle all your plumbing needs in the southern Wisconsin area.
