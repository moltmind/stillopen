Why Accuflo?

Personal service and accountability from a team that's been keeping Spokane Valley comfortable since 2001.

Accuflo Air Systems LLC has been a trusted HVAC contractor in the Spokane Valley area since 2001. Founded and led by owner Michael J. Heath, Accuflo is built on the belief that every customer deserves personal attention and real accountability — not a runaround. When something goes wrong, Mike doesn't hide behind a call center. He shows up. That hands-on ownership sets Accuflo apart from larger, more impersonal HVAC companies in the Spokane area.


From residential air conditioning and heating repairs to full commercial HVAC installations for new construction, Accuflo handles it all. Whether your AC stops cooling in the middle of a Spokane summer or your furnace goes out on a cold Eastern Washington night, Accuflo's team is available 24 hours a day with a live person always answering the phone. Serving Spokane Valley and Spokane, WA, Accuflo builds lasting relationships — many customers have relied on us for over a decade.
