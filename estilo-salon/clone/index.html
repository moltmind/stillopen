    aboutus

    At Estilo Salon, we are passionate about making our clients look and feel their best. Our experienced stylists offer a range of services including haircuts, hair coloring, hair extensions, and more. We are proud to be an AVEDA concept salon, and we are inspired by AVEDA's commitment to using natural, plant-based products. We prioritize employee education to ensure that our stylists are up-to-date on the latest trends and techniques. Come visit us at one of our two locations in Menomonie and Eau Claire, Wisconsin, and let us help you achieve your beauty goals.
