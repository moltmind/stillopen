Experience Unmatched Comfort with Ron's Northfield Refrigeration Heating & Air

Ron's Northfield Refrigeration Heating & Air is a trusted local and family-owned business, serving Northfield, MN and the surrounding areas. With over 40 years of experience in the HVAC industry, we have established a reputation for providing exceptional heating and air conditioning services to both residential and commercial customers.


We are fully licensed and insured, and we service all makes and models of HVAC equipment. All of our work is backed by our customer satisfaction guarantee.
