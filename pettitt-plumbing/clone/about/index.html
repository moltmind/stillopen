About Pettitt Plumbing Heating and Air Conditioning

Pettitt Plumbing, Heating, and Air Conditioning, Inc. began serving the Norfolk area in the early 1950’s. Lee Pettitt took over the family business as a one-man operation in 1955 to continue to provide quality service to NE Nebraska.

Today, Pettitt Plumbing, Heating, and Air Conditioning, Inc. remains family-owned and operated by Lee’s son, Ron Pettitt, and son-in-law, Dale Primose, Jr.

Along with their crew, Ron and Dale are committed to providing the Norfolk area with quality products and exceptional service.
