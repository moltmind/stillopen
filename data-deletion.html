Data Deletion

Last updated: November 3, 2026

You can ask me to delete your StillOpen data at any time. This page covers three groups:

  • Callers who phoned a shop and reached its after-hours desk.
  • Shop owners with a StillOpen account.
  • Facebook users whose Messenger conversations were handled by a StillOpen desk on a shop's Facebook Page.

Callers

How to ask: email cole@stillopen.ai with the subject "Delete my data," or call (920) 332-6113. Tell me the phone number you called from and about when you called.

What gets deleted: the record of your call, the transcript, the name, number and address you gave the desk, and any booking or callback request made from that call.

The shop's copy: the shop you called got your details by email. That copy is under the shop's control. I'll let the shop know you asked.

Shop owners

How to ask: email cole@stillopen.ai with the subject "Delete my data" and your business name, or call (920) 332-6113.

What gets deleted: your account record (name, email, sign-in), your desk's setup (business info, services, service area, hours, on-call number, rules), your call records and transcripts, booking and callback records, and stats. Stripe keeps payment records under its own rules, as needed for taxes and chargebacks. I'll ask Stripe to delete what it allows.

Facebook users

How to request deletion

Send an email to:

cole@stillopen.ai

with the subject line: Delete my data

Please include in the email:

  • Your name (and business name, if you are an account holder).
  • The email address associated with your StillOpen account, OR
  • The Facebook Page name and approximate date of the Messenger conversation, if you are an end user.

What gets deleted

For Facebook Messenger end users

  • Any conversation logs containing your Page-Scoped ID (PSID) on the StillOpen-operated infrastructure.
  • Any contact, address, or job-description information you shared with the bot.

Note: messages stored in your own Facebook Messenger inbox are controlled by Meta, not StillOpen. To delete those, use Facebook's own controls inside Messenger.

For business owners

  • All conversation logs from your website widget and connected Facebook Pages.
  • Connected integration tokens (Google Calendar refresh tokens, Facebook Page Access Tokens). The Page is also automatically unsubscribed from StillOpen's webhook.

Timeline

I delete within 30 days of your request and confirm by email or phone.

Self-service for business owners

You can also disconnect specific integrations at any time directly from your dashboard:

  • Disconnect Google Calendar from /app/dashboard.html → Integrations → Google Calendar.
  • Disconnect Facebook Page from /app/dashboard.html → Integrations → Facebook Page.

Disconnecting an integration removes the corresponding token from our system within minutes. Full account deletion still requires the email request above.
