One family.
One hundred and three years.

1923

Frank Sailer Sr. opens on Main Street.

Seventeen when he left Austria, he settled in Elmwood because the hills reminded him of home. A meat market with a full line of groceries, and a son, Frank Jr., born months after the doors opened.

THE LOCKER YEARS

Frank Jr. & Lorena build the plant.

Long hours turned the market into a full-service locker plant with its own slaughtering area, custom processing for farms across the county.

1995

Ric & Mary take the counter, with Jake.

The groceries went. The craft stayed. Custom processing and sausage making became the whole story.

2006

Outgrew the building. They built.

Jake designed the new plant on Winter Avenue himself: bigger coolers, more room, and a retail area worth the drive.

TODAY

Jake Sailer, fifth generation.

UW-Madison Master Meat Crafter, past president of the Wisconsin Association of Meat Processors, up since two most mornings. The sixth generation is already growing up in the shop.
