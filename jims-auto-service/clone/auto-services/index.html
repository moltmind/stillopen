Auto Services

Auto manufacturers equip all of their vehicles with computers and electrical components that control various aspects of your car. This makes old-fashioned, backyard service obsolete. Sophisticated equipment is necessary to run diagnostics on many late model vehicles. At Jim’s Auto Service, we have the equipment and expert technicians that will diagnose your problem quickly and repair your vehicle professionally every time.

We pride ourselves on being able to offer cost effective solutions to any car or truck problem you may encounter. With the knowledge and experience that our technicians possess, we are positive that your vehicle will be back on the road in no time. We only employ highly trained ASE-certified technicians.

Towing Services
