Welcome Jim's Auto Services

Welcome to Jim's Auto Service, your premier automotive repair and towing company. We have been providing Lake City, Florida, and the surrounding areas with quality services and affordable prices since May 1977. With over 65 years of combined knowledge and experience in the automotive industry, we are confident we will meet and exceed all of your expectations.
Some of Our Services Include:
  • Lock-out Service
  • Local and Long-Distance Towing
  • Brake Service
  • Alignments
  • And Much More…
Vehicles are more technologically advanced then ever. Many vehicles have electronic sensors that can measure everything from tire pressure to which wheel is slipping during a turn. With all of these technological advancements in automobiles, highly trained technicians are needed to make even the slightest adjustment or repair on your vehicle. We have the staff and the equipment to get the job done right.

If your car has left you stranded or is in need of repairs, please feel free to contact us at 386-758-9463 for repairs and 386-752-7305 for towing service. Our friendly and professional staff will be able to assist you with any questions or concerns you may have.
