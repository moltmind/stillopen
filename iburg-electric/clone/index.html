Our Process for Electrical Projects


Our electricians in Hudson, WI believe that a great electrical experience is built on clear communication, careful planning, and flawless execution. That’s why Iburg Electric follows a well-structured process for every job, whether it’s a single-room lighting upgrade or a full-scale wiring installation. Our method is designed to keep you informed, reduce surprises, and deliver results you’ll be happy with for years to come.


Initial Consultation and Assessment


Our process starts with an in-depth conversation to understand your needs and vision. We’ll listen carefully to your goals, whether you want to improve energy efficiency, enhance lighting, or troubleshoot a persistent electrical issue. From there, we visit your property to assess your existing electrical systems and evaluate any potential concerns. This step is crucial because it allows us to identify the safest and

most efficient path forward.


During this stage, our electricians in Hudson, WI take precise measurements, review your panel capacity, and note any code compliance issues that may need to be addressed. We’ll also provide recommendations that align with your budget and timeline so you can make informed decisions before any work begins.


Customized Project Planning


Once we’ve gathered all the necessary information, we move into the planning phase. Every property is unique, and our goal is to create a tailored approach that delivers exactly what you need. This could mean mapping out a new lighting layout, upgrading electrical panels, or planning the wiring for an addition or remodel.


Our detailed project plan includes clear timelines, a breakdown of materials, and a transparent cost estimate. You’ll know exactly what to expect, and our team will be available to answer any questions along the way. At Iburg Electric, we believe that great planning sets the foundation for great results, and our electricians in Hudson, WI work hard to anticipate challenges before they arise.


Professional Execution and Follow-Up


With the plan in place, our crew arrives on time, fully equipped, and ready to get to work. We take pride in our workmanship and maintain a clean, organized job site throughout the project. Safety is our top priority, so we follow strict safety protocols while using only reliable,

industry-approved materials.


Once the work is complete, we conduct a thorough inspection to verify that everything is functioning correctly and meets code requirements. Our electricians in Hudson, WI will walk you through the finished results, answer any final questions, and provide guidance on how to get the most out of your new electrical systems. Even after the project wraps up, Iburg Electric remains available for follow-up support, because we believe that lasting relationships are built on trust and reliability.


Service Areas

Over the years, we’ve built strong relationships with homeowners, business owners, and property managers across the region. Whether you’re in need of a quick repair, a major upgrade, or ongoing maintenance, our electricians in Hudson, WI bring the same attention to detail and customer care to every project.


Iburg Electric is dedicated to being a dependable partner for all your electrical needs—wherever you are in our service area.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can you tell me more about your background, and how long your team has been serving local clients?

    We’ve been delivering residential, commercial, farm, agricultural, and new-construction electrical services since 2005, backed by more than three decades of industry experience. As a locally and family-owned business, we take pride in offering workmanship you can trust — that’s why we include a seven-year workmanship warranty. When you contact us, you’re working with electricians in Hudson, WI who know the area and understand local building codes, giving you confidence that your project is handled by experienced and reliable professionals.

  • What kinds of electrical jobs do you handle for homes and businesses?

    We cover a wide variety of services — from basic repairs and safety inspections to complete rewiring, wiring installation, lighting upgrades, and even full electrical system installations for new homes, farms, or commercial buildings. Whether you want updated lighting, panel upgrades, code-compliant wiring, or more advanced systems like security wiring or appliance hookups, our electricians in Hudson, WI have the expertise to get the job done right.

  • If I’m planning a renovation or new build, can you help design the electrical system before installation?

    Yes — we offer custom electrical design services, creating wiring layouts and lighting plans that suit your space, usage, and aesthetic preferences. Our design-first approach ensures your system will be safe, efficient, and built to handle future demands. We work with electricians in Hudson, WI to deliver designs that maximize energy efficiency, integrate specialty systems (like spa wiring or security alarms), and meet all local code requirements.

  • How do you approach a project from the first call to final inspection?

    Our process begins with a thoughtful consultation and inspection to understand your needs and assess your current electrical system. Then we craft a customized project plan with a transparent estimate, material list, and timeline. Once you approve, our team arrives prepared, works safely and cleanly, and completes the job diligently. Finally, we conduct a detailed inspection to confirm everything is up to code and functioning properly — and we walk you through the results. Our electricians in Hudson, WI handle every step so you’re never left wondering what comes next.

  • Do you help with energy efficiency and future-proofing?

    Absolutely. We often recommend and install energy-efficient lighting, optimized circuit layouts, and smart wiring systems to support modern appliances, EV chargers, or increased loads. Using our design services and installation skills, we help you get the most efficient electrical setup possible. When you work with Iburg Electric, you get advice and implementation that balances safety, code compliance, and long-term energy savings.

  • Do you work with new construction, older homes, or farm/agricultural buildings?

    We serve all those — whether it’s a brand-new home or commercial building, a rural farm or agricultural property, or an older home needing rewiring or upgrades. From advanced circuit layouts to wiring for barns or outbuildings, our versatile team handles diverse electrical environments. With our experience, our electricians in Hudson, WI are equipped to understand the unique challenges of each setting and ensure every installation is safe and up to standard.

  • How do you stand out from other electrical contractors in the region?

    We believe what sets us apart is our combination of dependability, craftsmanship, and value. We’re fully licensed and insured in both Wisconsin and Minnesota, we offer free estimates typically within 24 hours, and we pledge to beat any valid written estimate. Because we specialize in many services — from residential and commercial to farms, new builds, EV charger installation, and custom design — you get a one-stop solution rather than juggling multiple contractors. Our electricians deliver consistent quality, clear communication, and long-lasting results that clients feel good about.

  • Do you offer guidance on choosing the right lighting or fixtures for my space?

    Yes! We’re happy to help you select lighting options that match your style, layout, and functional needs. We can explain the benefits of different fixture types, recommend placements for balanced illumination, and ensure everything is compatible with your electrical system so your space looks great and performs reliably.
