Services

Heating

Three Peaks Heating and Cooling makes it easy to stay cozy all winter long with reliable and knowledgeable heating services for your home or business. We have experience with all of your heating systems and components. From communicating equipment and geothermal systems to furnaces and thermostats, we have the experience and equipment to keep your home or business comfortable, cozy, and warm all winter long.
 
With the changing energy prices, there may be good reason to consider converting your furnace to a more efficient or cost effective fuel source. If you're considering making a change to your existing furnace to a new fuel type, we have the tools and experience to help you make the change or to help you find cost effective solutions to suit your needs.

Air Conditioning

During the hot, Oregon summers, the right air conditioning system will help to maintain the comfort and health of you and your family, employees, and customers. For those suffering from outdoor allergy symptoms, an air conditioning system helps bring a measure of relief. With  10+ years of experience in the heating and cooling business, Three Peaks Heating and Cooling will help select the right system to keep you cool, cozy, and comfortable, and to help you save money. Contact us today for more information or to get started.
