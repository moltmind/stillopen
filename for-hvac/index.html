Does my number change?

No. Same number on the truck, the cards and Google. If nobody picks up by the fourth ring, the call rolls over to the desk. You can turn the forwarding off whenever you want.

What does it do if somebody smells gas?

Before anything else, it says: "Get everyone out of the house right now. Don't flip any switches or use anything electric on the way out. Once you're outside, call the gas company or nine one one." That line is built in, so it goes first every time. Then it offers to connect them to your on-call phone, and it only transfers on a yes.

Does it tell a no-heat customer when I'll be there?

No. It takes the name, number and address, tells them they're first in line for the next opening, and says you'll call to confirm the time. It never gives an arrival window. You know where your trucks are. It doesn't.

Can the real emergencies go to my on-call phone?

Yes. A true emergency, like a gas smell, gets offered a transfer to your on-call phone, and it only goes through on a yes. Everything else gets on the list, and you call them back to set the time.

Does it speak Spanish?

It's built to answer in Spanish too. Spanish on the phone is newer than the English side, so call the demo line and try it before you count on it.

What's it cost, and what if it doesn't work?

$497 to go live and $197 a month. Cancel any time. You're live 48 hours after you pay. If it hasn't caught you a job in 30 days, the $497 comes back. No sales call.
