About Us
Beauje Hair Salon in Menomonie, WI, is your premier destination for outstanding hair services, catering to clients from Downsville, Durand, Elk Mound, Eau Claire, Chippewa Falls, and beyond. Our talented stylists boast years of experience and specialize in haircuts and trims for women, men, and children. Frustrated with your hair? We're here to solve your hair woes! Drop by or give us a call, if we miss you, just leave your name and number on our voicemail, and we’ll return your call promptly. Discover a fresh new look and personalized attention with your next haircut at Beauje Hair Salon in Menomonie. Join the talented team at Beauje Hair Salon! We're seeking an experienced part-time Hair Stylist with established clientele following. Enjoy the flexibility of hourly or commission pay (whichever is higher) - paid out weekly!
