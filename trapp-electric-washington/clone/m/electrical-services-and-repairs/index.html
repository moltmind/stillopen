Reliable Electrical Services for Your Home or Business

When the lights flicker or your outlets stop working, you need an electrician you can trust. That's where Trapp Electric comes in. We've been serving the Peoria, IL area since 2001, bringing over a century of combined experience to every job. Our team of skilled electricians is ready to tackle any electrical issue, big or small. We're not just another electrical company – we're your neighbors, committed to keeping your home or business safe and powered up.

At Trapp Electric, we pride ourselves on our commitment to quality work and customer satisfaction. As a veteran-owned and operated business, we understand the importance of integrity and reliability. We're certified Tesla Energy installers and Generac experts, so you know you're getting the best when it comes to cutting-edge energy solutions. Plus, we've been recognized with the Community Votes Peoria Platinum award, a testament to our dedication to excellence. Whether you need a simple repair or a complete electrical overhaul, we're here to help. Don't let electrical problems keep you in the dark – reach out to Trapp Electric today for a free estimate and experience the difference our expertise can make.

Circuit Breaker Services

Is your circuit breaker constantly tripping? It might be time for a repair or replacement. Our electricians can diagnose the issue and provide a swift, effective solution. We'll ensure your electrical system is properly protected, preventing potential hazards and keeping your power running smoothly.

Electrical Outlet & Light Switches

Our expert team is here to ensure your outlets and light switches are in top shape. We specialize in repairing or replacing damaged outlets, installing GFCI outlets in moisture-prone areas, and adding new outlets to accommodate your expanding electrical needs. Let us make sure your electrical outlets and switches are safe, functional, and ready to keep up with your lifestyle.

Electrical Wiring Services
