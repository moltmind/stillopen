Powering Peoria With Reliable Generator Solutions

When the lights go out, Trapp Electric's got your back. We're the Peoria area's go-to electricians for all things generators, bringing over a century of combined expertise to every job. Since 2001, we've been keeping homes and businesses humming, even when the grid takes a nap. Our team doesn't just install generators; we're Generac-certified pros who know these machines inside and out. And it's not just about slapping a generator in your yard—we're talking full-service installation, maintenance, and repairs that'll keep you powered up when it matters most.

But here's the kicker: we're not just another electrical outfit. We're your neighbors, proud members of the Washington Chamber of Commerce, and a veteran-owned business that takes pride in serving our community. We've earned our stripes as Community Votes Peoria Platinum winners, and we're not stopping there. From competitive pricing to our rock-solid craftsmanship guarantee, we're all about giving you peace of mind. And did we mention we're Tesla energy-certified? Yeah, we're that cutting-edge. So whether you're looking to keep your lights on during the next storm or want to upgrade your home's electrical system, give Trapp Electric a buzz. We'll show you why Peoria trusts us to keep the power flowing.
