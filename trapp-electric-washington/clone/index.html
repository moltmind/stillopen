Powering Peoria with Expert Electrical Solutions

Since 2001, Trapp Electric has been lighting up homes and businesses across the Peoria area with our unparalleled electrical expertise. We're not just another electrical contractor; we're your neighbors, committed to keeping our community safe and powered up. With over a century of combined experience, our team of skilled electricians tackles everything from minor repairs to major installations with precision and care.

As a veteran-owned and operated business, we bring discipline, integrity, and a strong work ethic to every job. We're proud members of the Washington Chamber of Commerce and have been recognized as Community Votes Peoria Platinum winners. Our commitment to excellence extends beyond our work – we're investing in the future of our trade through our nationally recognized apprenticeship program. Whether you need a simple fix or a complete electrical overhaul, we've got you covered with competitive pricing and a craftsmanship guarantee. Ready to brighten up your space? Contact us for a free estimate today!

Electrical Repairs

Got a flickering light or a finicky outlet? Don't sweat it. Our team specializes in all kinds of electrical repairs, from circuit breakers to wiring issues. We'll diagnose the problem quickly and fix it right the first time, so you can get back to your day without any shocking surprises.

EV Charger Installation

Jumping on the electric vehicle bandwagon? Smart move! We're experts at installing EV chargers, making sure you can power up your ride right at home. As a Tesla Energy Certified Installer, we'll set you up with the latest charging technology, keeping you on the road and off the grid.

Generators

Don't let power outages leave you in the dark. As Generac-certified pros, we offer top-of-the-line generator installation and maintenance. We'll help you choose the right generator for your needs and keep it running smoothly, so you're always prepared for whatever Mother Nature throws your way.

Pool and Hot Tub Wiring

Ready to take a dip? Let us handle the pool and hot tub wiring. We'll ensure your aquatic oasis is safely and properly powered, so you can relax and enjoy without worrying about electrical hazards. From underwater lighting to pump systems, we've got all your pool and spa electrical needs covered.

Residential New Construction and Remodels

Building your dream home or giving your current one a facelift? We're here to make sure the electrical side of things is absolutely perfect.
