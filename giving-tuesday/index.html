Subject: The schoolhouse needs 60 of us on Tuesday

Dear friends,

Next Tuesday, December 1, is Giving Tuesday, and we have one job for it: keep the 1887 Mill Road schoolhouse open to fourth graders next spring.

Every May, about 300 local kids spend a morning at those desks with slates and a hand bell. The roof over the cloakroom will not make it through another winter without repair, and the estimate is $1,500.

So here is the plan. Sixty neighbors, $25 each, one day. That is the whole campaign.

You can give any time on Tuesday at our donation page. If you would rather mail a check, our address is at the bottom of this note.

Thank you for keeping the bell ringing.

Margaret Olsen
Board President, River Valley Historical Society
