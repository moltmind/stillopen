About Us

About Us

Under Aire Heating and Cooling is a family owned business serving Barron County since 2019.

Shane Larson is originally from Menomonie, WI and received his degree in HVAC from Brevard County, FL. After working for other Heating & Cooling companies for over 25 years, he often felt his employers cared more about themselves than the customers. Shane has always enjoyed helping others out. Even after becoming a Volunteer Firefighter and then an EMT he still felt he needed to do more.

​Renee grew up in Birchwood, WI on a dairy farm most of her life. After the couple lost a very special man in their lives (Thomas Sirinek, Renee’s father) they decided to move back to the area to be closer to Nancy Crotteau/Sirinek (Renee’s mother).

In 2019 the couple invested their life savings into pursuing the dream of owning their own business, Under Aire Heating & Cooling. Renee continues teaching at Hilltop Elementary while helping with the business when she can.

Shane runs service calls and does installs. The couple’s two children even help with the business on the weekends! They feel important when they have a broom in their hand or help organize equipment.
