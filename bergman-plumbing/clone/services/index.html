Serving the Greater Fort Dodge community

Bergman Plumbing takes care of a wide variety of plumbing issues for homeowners and commercial businesses. From the smallest leak to installing the plumbing systems for over 60 motels, Bergman Plumbing has many years of experience.

Bergman Plumbing repairs and/or replaces:

  • water heaters
  • water softeners
  • faucets, sinks, toilets
  • garbage disposals
  • sump pumps
  • other miscellaneous leaks and plumbing problems

We install gas lines for fireplaces, dryers, and stoves, etc. We also do the plumbing for kitchen and bath remodels, as well as, for new homes. We have been the plumbing contractor for building over 60 motels throughout Iowa for almost 20 years.

Bergman Plumbing is available to clean your drain line and/or your sewer line. We have a video sewer camera to inspect your sewer line for issues with plugged sewers and if needed, we are available to dig up your sewer line and repair it. We are licensed, bonded, and insured.
