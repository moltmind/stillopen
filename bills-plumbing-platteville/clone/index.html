About Us
Bill’s Plumbing & Heating, based in Platteville, WI, has been the area’s go-to provider for reliable plumbing and HVAC services since 1995. Proudly serving a 35-mile radius, we bring decades of experience to every residential and light commercial project—delivering expert installation, prompt repairs, and long-lasting comfort. Whether it’s fixing a leak, upgrading your boiler, or installing a full heating and cooling system, our dedicated team ensures efficient service with a personal touch. For trusted craftsmanship and hometown care, count on Bill’s Plumbing & Heating to keep your home and business running smoothly.

Services
  • Heating and Air Conditioning – Stay comfortable year-round with expert HVAC solutions. We service, install, and maintain heating and cooling systems to keep your indoor environment efficient and reliable in every season.
  • Boilers – Our team provides specialized care for boiler systems, including installation, maintenance, and repairs—ensuring optimal performance and consistent heat when you need it most.
  • Installation and Repair – Whether you're updating equipment or fixing a sudden issue, we offer prompt, professional installation and repair services across a wide range of systems and products.
  • Residential and Light Commercial – Serving homeowners and small businesses alike, we deliver tailored solutions designed to meet the unique demands of residential properties and light commercial facilities.
  • Septic – Trust our experts for septic system services, including inspections, maintenance, and repairs. We ensure your system runs smoothly and meets environmental standards.
  • Water Softener – Improve water quality and appliance longevity with our water softener installation and servicing. Say goodbye to hard water buildup and enjoy cleaner, better-tasting water.
  • Drain Cleaning – Fast and effective drain cleaning to eliminate blockages, improve flow, and prevent future plumbing issues. We handle everything from kitchen sinks to main sewer lines.
  • Saturdays by Appointment – Need weekend service? We offer Saturday appointments for your convenience, giving you added flexibility without sacrificing quality or attention.

FAQ
What services does Bill’s Plumbing & Heating offer?
We provide expert plumbing and HVAC services including heating and cooling system installation and repair, boiler maintenance, septic system care, water softener installation, and drain cleaning. We serve both residential and light commercial clients.
What areas do you serve?
We proudly serve Platteville, WI and the surrounding communities within a 35-mile radius.
Do you offer weekend appointments?
Yes, we offer Saturday appointments by request to provide added flexibility for your busy schedule.
Can you help with septic systems and water quality?
Absolutely. We offer septic system inspections, maintenance, and repairs, as well as water softener installation and servicing to improve water quality and protect your plumbing.
How long has Bill’s Plumbing & Heating been in business?
We’ve been serving the community since 1995, bringing over 30 years of experience to every project with reliable service and hometown care.
