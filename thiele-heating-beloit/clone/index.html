Who We Are

Family owned and operated since 1983

Thiele Heating & Air Conditioning is a BBB accredited business that was established in 1983 by Roger and Peggy Thiele. For over 30 years, we have provided heating, air conditioning, and refrigeration services to residential homes and local businesses throughout Southern Wisconsin and Northern Illinois. 

Our highly trained technicians have substantial HVAC experience and provide quality workmanship for a variety of heating and cooling equipment brands. 

  • 30+ years of quality service and experience.
  • Skilled team of technicians with superior workmanship. 
  • Cost efficient heating and cooling solutions of top brands.
  • Excellent customer service.
