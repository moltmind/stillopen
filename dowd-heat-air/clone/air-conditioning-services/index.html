Know the Signs

When to Call for AC Service

Don't wait until your AC completely breaks down. These warning signs mean it's time to call Dowd Heat and Air.

AC is blowing warm air

Could indicate low refrigerant, a failed compressor, or a broken condenser fan.

Water leaking around the unit

A clogged drain line or frozen evaporator coil could be causing water damage to your home.

System cycles on and off frequently

Short cycling wears out your compressor and wastes energy. Usually a sign of an oversized unit or thermostat issue.

Unusual smells from vents

Musty odors can mean mold in your ductwork. A burning smell could indicate an electrical problem.

System is over 10–15 years old

Older units lose efficiency and use outdated refrigerants. A new system could cut your energy bills significantly.

Don't Wait Until It Breaks

Catching AC problems early saves you money and prevents a full breakdown when you need cooling the most.

AC Questions Answered

Air Conditioning FAQ

How quickly can you get to my home for an AC emergency?

We offer same-day emergency AC service across the Tulsa metro area. In most cases, we can have a technician at your home within a few hours of your call. Give us a call at (918) 437-3721 and we'll dispatch someone as quickly as possible.

How much does AC repair cost in Tulsa?

AC repair costs vary depending on the issue — from a simple capacitor replacement to a compressor swap. We always provide an upfront diagnosis and quote before any work begins, so you know exactly what to expect. No hidden fees, ever.

Should I repair or replace my air conditioner?

As a general rule, if your AC is under 10 years old and the repair cost is less than half the price of a new unit, repair is usually the better option. If it's over 15 years old, uses R-22 refrigerant, or needs frequent repairs, a replacement will save you money long-term. We'll give you an honest recommendation — we never push a replacement unless it's truly the smarter move.

What AC brands do you install?

We install and service all major brands including Rheem, Carrier, Trane, Bryant, Goodman, and Lennox. We'll help you choose the right system based on your home's size, your budget, and your efficiency goals.

How often should I have my AC serviced?

We recommend a professional AC tune-up at least once a year, ideally in the spring before Oklahoma's hot season kicks in. This helps catch small issues early, maintains efficiency, and can extend the life of your system by several years. Our maintenance plans make it easy to stay on schedule.

Do you offer financing for a new AC system?

Absolutely. We partner with Upgrade to offer flexible financing options so you can get the air conditioning system your home needs without paying the full amount upfront. Ask us about available plans when we provide your estimate.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Several things can cause this: a dirty air filter restricting airflow, low refrigerant from a leak, a frozen evaporator coil, or a failing compressor. Start by checking and replacing your filter. If that doesn't help, give us a call and we'll diagnose the issue quickly.
