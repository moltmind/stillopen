Common Questions

Contact FAQs

How quickly will you respond to my message?

We aim to respond to all contact form submissions within 1 business hour during our operating hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–5pm). For after-hours emergencies, calling (918) 437-3721 is the fastest way to reach us.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?

Yes! HVAC emergencies don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. If your heat or AC goes out unexpectedly, call us directly at (918) 437-3721 and we'll dispatch a technician as quickly as possible.

Is there a charge for the initial estimate?

We provide upfront quotes before beginning any work, so you'll always know the cost before we start. For new system installations, we're happy to come out and provide a free estimate. Call us or fill out the form above to get started.

Do you offer financing for larger projects?

Absolutely. We partner with Upgrade to offer flexible financing so you can get the comfort system your home needs without paying the full amount upfront. Apply for financing online or ask us about available plans when you contact us.

What should I have ready when I call?

It helps to know your system's make and model (usually on a label on the unit), a brief description of the issue, and your availability for a service visit. But don't worry if you don't have all that — we'll walk you through everything.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. We also offer financing through Upgrade for larger installations and system replacements.
