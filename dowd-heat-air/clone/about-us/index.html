Our Story

Built on Hard Work & Honest Service

Back in 1995, Terrence Dowd Sr. started Dowd Heat and Air with a simple belief: Tulsa families deserve HVAC service they can actually trust. No gimmicks. No pressure. Just professional workmanship and honest answers.

Three decades later, that belief hasn't changed. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a full team of certified technicians — but we've never lost the personal touch that makes us different. When you call Dowd, you're not getting a call center or a franchise. You're getting a local family business that knows your neighborhood and takes pride in every single job.

Today, Dowd Heat and Air serves 14+ communities across the Tulsa metro, with a 4.9-star Google rating and 580+ five-star reviews. We're proud of those numbers — but we're even prouder that most of our business comes from repeat customers and referrals.
