Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What HVAC services does Dowd Heat and Air offer?

We offer a complete range of HVAC services including air conditioning repair, installation, and maintenance; heating and furnace services; whole-home air filtration and purification; smart thermostat installation (including EcoNet); and convenient maintenance plans. Whether it's an emergency repair or a planned installation, we've got you covered.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?

Yes! We understand that HVAC emergencies don't wait for business hours. We offer fast-response emergency service across the Tulsa metro area. Give us a call at (918) 437-3721 and we'll get a technician to your home as quickly as possible.

What brands do you install and service?

We install and service all major HVAC brands including Rheem, Carrier, Trane, Bryant, Goodman, and Lennox. Our technicians are trained and certified across multiple manufacturers, so regardless of your current system, we can help.

Do you offer financing for new system installations?

Absolutely. We partner with Upgrade to offer flexible financing options so you can get the comfort system your home needs without paying the full amount upfront. Ask us about available plans when we provide your quote.

How often should I have my HVAC system serviced?

We recommend servicing your system twice a year — once in the spring for cooling prep and once in the fall for heating prep. Our maintenance plans make this easy and help extend the life of your equipment, prevent costly breakdowns, and keep your energy bills in check.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Tulsa metro area including Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Owasso, Sand Springs, Sapulpa, Claremore, Collinsville, Glenpool, Skiatook, Coweta, Bristow, and Tahlequah. Not sure if you're in our area? Just give us a call!
