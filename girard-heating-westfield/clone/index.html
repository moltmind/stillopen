Frequently Asked Questions

Get informed!

What is a heat pump?

A heat pump is a high-efficiency system that heats and cools your home using electricity. Instead of creating heat, it moves heat in or out of your home depending on the season. One system does it all—comfort year-round with lower energy use.

Is Springfield a good area for heat pumps?

Yes. Homes in Springfield are well-suited for modern cold-climate heat pumps like Mitsubishi’s Hyper Heat system.  These systems are designed to perform efficiently through New England’s changing seasons, including winter cold and summer heat.

Are there rebates for heat pumps?

Yes. Massachusetts homeowners may qualify for generous incentives through Mass Save. Rebates can significantly reduce the upfront cost of installing a heat pump system, especially when replacing older heating equipment. Mitsubishi has many heat pumps that qualify for rebates and incentives.

Do heat pumps work in cold climates?

Absolutely. Today’s cold-climate heat pumps are engineered to deliver reliable heat even when outdoor temperatures drop well below freezing. Mitsubishi has a broad selection of high efficiency heat pumps that have proven to be effective in Massachusetts winters.

Can a heat pump replace my furnace?

In many homes, yes. A properly designed heat pump system can fully replace a traditional furnace while providing more consistent comfort and improved efficiency. When you choose a heat pump you get the added benefit of air conditioning because a heat pump can both heat and cool.

Do heat pumps provide air conditioning?

Yes. Heat pumps provide efficient air conditioning in the summer by removing heat from your home. You get quiet, even cooling without needing a separate AC system. And when the weather cools down, you can rely on your heat pump to provide heating all winter long.
