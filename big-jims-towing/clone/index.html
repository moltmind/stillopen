Affordable Spokane Towing Services

With decades of experience towing in and around the Spokane area (and North Idaho too) it’s no wonder so many local residents and businesses call Big Jim’s Towing when they need a tow truck. Our unmatched dedication to friendly, safe, reliable, and affordable towing is just how we do business.

Great Tow Trucks, Great Service, Great Prices

Our tow trucks are top of the line, run by skilled experts who take pride in helping our community with affordable and friendly towing services. Big Jim’s is local, and we’ve got a long list of local customers who know when they need a tow truck, they can count on us. So, give us a call and experience next level towing services.

In Spokane, Spokane Valley, Liberty Lake, Coeur d’Alene, Post Falls Call Big Jim’s Towing for the very best towing and roadside assistance in the Inland Northwest.

Spokane Towing

As a top Spokane towing company, Big Jim’s Towing is here to help you out when you need a tow truck in Spokane county. We’ve got years of experience with local flatbed tow services. Our dedication to friendly prompt and affordable towing in unmatched in the Greater Spokane area.

Jump Starts

Dead Battery? we’ll bring you the power to get back on the road again. With Jumpstart serv ices from Big Jim’s Towing we can hook up your car, truck or SUV and get you running again right away. If it turns out there is a bigger problem, and your vehicle won’t take a jump we can also help with towing to your local mechanic at a great price.

Lockouts

Almost everyone has locked themselves out of their car at some point, but don’t worry because Big Jim’s Towing provides affordable on call lockout services across Spokane and Kootenai counties. We’re just a phone call away and ready to help you get back on the road again.

Flatbed Towing services from Big Jim’s Are Ideal for modified, lowered, or damaged vehicles, long distance towing, accident removal, vintage/collectable car transportation and unfavorable weather/road conditions. Rest assured when you call Big Jim’s towing your calling the local flatbed towing experts.

Tow Truck Services

Need a Tow Truck? Call Big Jim’s because we’ve got flatbeds, wheel lifts and hook and chain tow trucks to help you get where you need to. We’re just a phone call away with the tow truck you need, when you need it.
