Frequently Asked Questions 

  • How do I know if my air conditioner needs repair or replacement?

     If your AC is frequently breaking down, inefficient, or over 10-15 years old, it might be time to consider replacement. Our technicians can evaluate your system and recommend the best option.

  • How often should I schedule air conditioner maintenance?

    We recommend professional AC maintenance at least once a year, preferably before the cooling season starts, to keep your system working efficiently.

  • Can you install energy-efficient air conditioning units?

     Absolutely! We offer a range of high-efficiency air conditioners that can save you money on energy bills while keeping your home cool.

  • What brands do you service and install?

    We service and install all major HVAC brands including Carrier, Lennox, Trane, Goodman, and more.

  • How long does an AC installation take?

    Most installations are completed within one day, depending on the complexity of the system and your home’s setup.

  • Do you offer emergency repair services?

    Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency air conditioning repair to ensure you’re never left without cooling during Knoxville’s hot days.
