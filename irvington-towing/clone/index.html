About Us
Irvington Garage Towing has been Menomonie’s trusted towing and auto repair service since 1947. We proudly serve Menomonie, Downsville, Elmwood, Boyceville, Durand, and nearby communities with fast, reliable towing and roadside assistance. Our services include 24/7 emergency towing, local and long-distance towing, welding, radiator repair, large truck service, auto maintenance, and transmission solutions.
