About Us

Learn More(603) 527-8680

Over 40 Years of Combined Plumbing Excellence

Mountain Plumbing of New Hampshire is a father and son family owned plumbing company with over 40 years of combined experience in the field.

Our team brings a depth of knowledge and expertise in all aspects of plumbing, from repairs and maintenance in single family homes to large-scale commercial builds and apartment complexes.

Our team specializes in plumbing and heating for both commercial and residential new construction in the Granite State and service work in the Lakes Region of New Hampshire.

When you choose Mountain Plumbing of New Hampshire,  you will be receiving the highest level of expertise and quality in the plumbing industry. Contact us today to see how we can help with all of your plumbing needs.

Meet Our Team

Michael Irwin

Co-Owner

Michael is a 2nd generation plumber who grew up in the plumbing industry with 20 years of experience in the field. Starting in the fast paced Florida construction industry, Michael brought his experiences to New Hampshire to provide practical plumbing solutions in the Lakes Region of New Hampshire in 2018. Michael currently holds a Master Plumbing license, Gasfitters license, and Medical Gas license.
