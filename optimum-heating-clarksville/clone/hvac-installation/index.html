When It's Time to Replace, Not Repair

A repair that just delays the inevitable isn't a good deal. Here's when replacement is the smarter call.

  • System is 12–15+ years old with climbing repair costs

  • Still running on phased-out R-22 refrigerant

  • Energy bills keep climbing despite regular maintenance

  • Comfort is uneven and no repair has ever fixed it

  • You've had multiple repairs in the past 12 months

  • New construction or an addition with no ductwork in place

HVAC Installation & Replacement — Frequently Asked Questions

How do you decide what size system I need?

We run an on-site Manual J load calculation for every installation — the industry-standard method that accounts for your home's square footage, insulation, windows, and layout. No rule-of-thumb guessing, no oversized units that short-cycle.

How long does a typical HVAC replacement take?

Most residential system replacements are completed in a single day, from removal of the old equipment to startup and testing of the new system.

Do you install ductless mini-split systems?

Yes — see our ductwork & mini-split systems page for details on zoned comfort without major duct work.

What brands do you install?

We install all major residential and commercial HVAC brands and can recommend the right fit for your budget and home.

Can you handle HVAC for new construction or an addition?

Yes — we handle new-construction HVAC from the ground up, including full ductwork design, load calculations, and equipment selection before drywall goes up.
