Liberty Heating & Cooling ~ Veteran Owned Business

We provide solutions, replacements and repairs on all makes and models of furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps and ductless mini split heat pump systems for South Central Wisconsin. We are a small family owned business in Reedsburg, Wisconsin. We created Liberty with the idea of maintaining small overhead to allow for affordable prices. We wanted to create a company where we could treat our customers like our friends and family with care and courtesy, professional and timely service.

"I am a 23 year veteran of the US Air Force.  During my time in the Military, I was professionally trained in HVAC systems and Mechanical Engineering.  For over 20 years I've been provided heating and cooling solutions for commercial and residential systems. My knowledge of mechanical systems is superior. I enjoy trouble shooting systems, installing new equipment and helping people find HVAC solutions that solve their heating and cooling needs while staying within their budget.  I provide full-service furnace repair/replacement and ac repair/replacement. It has always been my dream to own my own business where I can provide my customers with personal care and professional service at affordable pricing."

Chamuel Marvici
