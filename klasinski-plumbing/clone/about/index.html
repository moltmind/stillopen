About Us

Jaime Klasinski started his plumbing career in 1981 working as an apprentice and then a journeyman plumber. After the required 8 years he was eligible to write the master plumbers exam.

Upon earning his master plumbers license, he founded Klasinski Plumbing and Heating in January of 1989. A short time later Jaime added a plumbing designer credential to allow him to design plumbing systems for commercial buildings.

In 2016, Jaime’s son Tyler started his plumbing career as an apprentice at Klasinski Plumbing. After earning his journeyman’s license, he is now working towards becoming a master plumber and taking control of the company over the next few years.
