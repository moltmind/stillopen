Wellness & Emergency Vet Care in Eau Claire, WI

For over 40 years, Oakwood Hills Animal Hospital in Eau Claire, WI, has proudly provided outstanding veterinary care for your pets. We offer full-service pet healthcare and preventive care to keep your companions in their best health. Our clinic is accredited through the AAHA.

Oakwood Hills Veterinary Hospital is staffed and equipped to handle the majority of emergencies and urgent care needs your pets may have, so we are here when you need us. Connect with our team of veterinary experts today by calling (715) 835-0112 or visit us at our clinic in Eau Claire!
