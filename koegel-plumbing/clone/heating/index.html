Heating Services

Heating Services

With sub-freezing temperatures for more of the year than we’d probably like, a well-functioning heating system is a necessity for survival here in Alexandria, MN. Hydronic heat (heating with water) is the most comfortable and efficient way to heat your home or building. Whether you are interested in repairing or improving an existing boiler system or installing a new residential or commercial boiler system, Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions in Alexandria, MN specializes in troubleshooting existing systems or designing new systems. There are a variety of heating sources for hydronic heating including natural gas, propane, electric, wood boilers, & even solar heating by using the sun! At Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions hydronic heat is our specialty. Whether you are having trouble with your 100-year-old gravity system or your modern in-floor heating system, you can rest well with Koegel!

Boiler Repairs, Installation & Maintenance Solutions

Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions has over 35 years of plumbing and heating experience, and we are well able to repair, install, or maintain a variety of types of boilers.

  • Standard Efficiency Boilers are boilers that are 78% to 89% efficient.
  • High Efficiency Boilers are boilers that are 90% – 98% efficient; has an electronic display and can be accessed with a smart phone.
  • Combination (Combi) Boilers are a combination of a high efficiency boiler and a small water tank that heats your domestic water and your home from one compact unit that hangs on the wall; about 95% efficient for both the boiler and the water tank.
  • Electric Boilers use electricity and require no chimney or fuel piping; usually wall mounted.
  • Natural Gas Boilers use natural gas as its heating source.
  • Propane Boilers run on propane gas.

Boiler System Diagnostics, Repairs, and Installation

Unsure which kind you have or which might be a better fit for you? No problem! You can rest well with Koegel! We have boiler system diagnostics to help you assess your situation. If you need a repair on your current system or are ready to install an upgrade, we have the experience and expertise to help you make a cost-effective decision. Call us today and we’ll help you plan your next steps.
