About Us

Our goal is to exceed your service expectations.

WHO WE ARE

Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions (pronounced Ko-gehl) is a fully licensed, bonded and insured residential plumbing, heating and water treatment company specializing in repair and installation services.  The business was founded in 2011 by Kurt Koegel, who was later joined in the business by his wife Dana Koegel. Kurt grew up in a plumbing family and brings 35 years of experience and a Master Plumber designation – the highest in the industry – to the table. Dana, a former Quality Control nurse, adds empathy and attention to detail to the team. As a result, Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions approaches plumbing, heating, and water treatment with a care for homeowners you won’t often find. Located in Miltona, MN, Koegel Plumbing & Heating Solutions services Alexandria, MN, and the surrounding lakes and communities of Douglas County.

THE KOEGEL PROMISE

Upfront Pricing

You will receive a written proposal that outlines in detail all work to be completed, along with a quoted price that will not change after the work has been done. You can feel 100% confident there will never be any pricing surprises.

Flexible Scheduling

Our flexible scheduling service means your schedule will always be our top priority. We know life can be busy, so we will call before your scheduled service date just to confirm it still works for you.

Clean, Courteous, & Professional

Our technicians will always use drop cloths and wear boot covers to protect the surfaces in your home and keep it clean. Many times, we’ve left it so clean that our customers asked if we were even there at all. To us, being courteous starts by respecting a customer’s time. We call ahead, we stick to our schedule, and we show up on time. As licensed, insured professionals, we don’t do short cuts or band-aids. That’s just not professional. So we promise that all plumbing, heating, and water treatment services will be performed by a professional, licensed plumber overseen by a Master Plumber with over 35 years in the industry.

The Extra Mile

Whether it’s our cabin opening in the spring or cabin closing in the fall – we believe in exceeding your expectations with our winterization services for lake homes and our contractor scheduling services for remodeling or additions. We even purchased our own excavation equipment so we don’t have to subcontract digging jobs on your project. That saves you time and money because we can control when the equipment is available, and there’s no hidden markup on the job. We know these are not services that most plumbers offer, but we want you to rest well with Koegel.
