    Get Your Heating and Cooling System Back on Track

    When your heating or cooling system breaks down, you need fast, dependable repairs to restore comfort to your home. C & E Heating & Air Conditioning Inc provides complete HVAC repair solutions for all makes and models. Our skilled technicians diagnose and fix issues quickly, making sure your system runs efficiently year-round. Serving Athens, TN, and the surrounding areas, we handle everything from minor adjustments to major component replacements.


    Reach out to us to schedule your repair service.

    Signs Your HVAC System Needs Repair
    Recognizing when your system needs attention can prevent costly breakdowns and maintain indoor comfort. Issues can arise from worn components, lack of maintenance, or age-related wear. Common warning signs include:
    • Unusual noises like grinding, banging, or squealing
    • Inconsistent temperatures throughout your home
    • Rising energy bills without increased usage
    • Weak airflow from vents
    • Frequent cycling on and off
    • Strange odors when the system runs

    Addressing these issues promptly helps avoid complete system failure and extends the life of your equipment. Serving Athens, TN, and the surrounding areas, our technicians provide thorough diagnostics and effective repairs.
    Why Choose C & E Heating & Air Conditioning
    With more than five decades of experience, we've built a reputation for quality workmanship and customer care. Our family-owned business has been serving local homeowners since 1974, and we understand the urgency of HVAC problems. We offer advantages that set us apart:
    • Complimentary estimates on repair work
    • Flexible financing options available
    • Recognition as a 2023 top performer
    • All manufacturer warranties honored
    • Extended warranty plans for added protection
    • Authorized Amana dealer with specialized training
    • Active participation in TBA EnergyRight programs

    Our commitment to excellence means you receive dependable service every time. We stand behind our repairs with superior customer support.
    Contact Us

    A malfunctioning HVAC system can disrupt your daily life and leave your home uncomfortable. Our locally owned and operated company is ready to restore your comfort with efficient, affordable repairs. Our certified technicians have the skills to handle any challenge, from emergency service to routine maintenance. Serving Athens, TN, and the surrounding areas, we're here when you need us most.


    Contact us today for your HVAC repair needs.
