About C & E Heating & Air Conditioning Inc

Since 1974, C & E Heating & Air Conditioning Inc has been the trusted name for comprehensive heating and cooling solutions throughout Athens, Tennessee. As a locally owned and family-operated business with over 50 years of combined experience, we've built our reputation on delivering premium customer service and expert HVAC installation, repairs, and replacements that keep homes comfortable year-round. Our commitment to excellence hasn't gone unnoticed—we're proud recipients of the Outstanding Sales Excellence Award and were voted among the Top 12 HVAC providers in 2023. As a certified Amana dealer, we honor all manufacturer warranties and offer extended warranty options for your peace of mind. Whether you need a complete system changeout or routine maintenance, our experienced technicians bring unmatched expertise to every service call, ensuring your home's climate control operates at peak efficiency.


Understanding that HVAC investments can be significant, C & E Heating & Air Conditioning makes comfort affordable through flexible financing options and our long-term participation in the TVA EnergyRight Financing Program. We specialize in both residential heating and cooling services, offering seasonal HVAC maintenance programs designed to extend your system's lifespan and prevent costly breakdowns. Our spring and winter maintenance services ensure your equipment runs smoothly during peak usage seasons, saving you money on energy bills while maintaining optimal indoor air quality. From emergency repairs to planned system replacements, we provide free estimates so you can make informed decisions about your home comfort needs. When you choose C & E Heating & Air Conditioning, you're partnering with a family business that treats your home with the same care and attention we'd give our own, backed by five decades of proven expertise in the Athens community.
