Why Is Regular Septic Pumping So Important?

Your septic system isn’t designed to last forever — but regular pumping and inspections can significantly extend its life and help you avoid costly repairs.

Neglecting maintenance can lead to clogged pipes, failed drainfields, and even total system replacement — which can cost up to $25,000.

In Wisconsin, state regulations require septic systems to be serviced at least once every 3 years. Ignoring that can result in fines, system damage, and health hazards.

With prompt service from 79 Septic, you’ll protect your property, stay compliant, and keep everything flowing properly — no backups, no mess.

People often ask what significance the number Seventy-Nine has to our company. The business has been operated by our family since the 1940s, and the original Wisconsin Sanitary License Number was 79 — so the name was chosen to honor that history.
