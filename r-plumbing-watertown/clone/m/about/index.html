About R Plumbing Inc

For more than 20 years, we have proudly provided dependable plumbing services at R Plumbing Inc to homeowners and businesses throughout the Watertown, WI, area. As a locally and family-owned and -operated company with a family presence in the community spanning the last 100 years, we are committed to delivering one-of-a-kind quality plumbing installation backed by honesty, integrity, and a true passion for the plumbing trade. We are fully insured and bonded, giving our customers confidence and peace of mind with every project. Our team offers a full range of residential and commercial plumbing services, including drain and pipe services, drain and pump services, gas line services, outdoor faucet installation, septic system services, specialized plumbing services, water heater services, and water treatment services. We take pride in building lasting relationships through reliable workmanship, personalized service, and solutions tailored to our customers’ needs. We are also proud to offer military, veteran, and senior discounts as a way of giving back to those who have served our community and country. Contact us today to schedule service.
