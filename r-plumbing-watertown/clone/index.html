Frequently Asked Questions

  • Are your plumbers insured and bonded?

    We're fully insured and bonded to provide complete peace of mind for our customers. This means your property is protected while our team works on your plumbing systems. We take our professional responsibilities seriously and maintain all necessary coverage.


    Along with our insurance and bonding, we bring over two decades of experience to every job. Our family-owned business operates with honesty, integrity, and a genuine passion for the plumbing trade. As the go-to plumbers Watertown, WI residents depend on, we deliver high-quality craftsmanship that solves your problems the first time.

  • What types of water treatment systems can you install?

    We install water treatment solutions to improve the water quality in your home. Our technicians frequently install water softening systems to address hard water issues. We also specialize in setting up iron filters to remove excess minerals from your supply.


    If you want comprehensive coverage, we can install a whole-house water filtration system. These systems ensure that clean water flows from every tap in your residence. We'll help you select the right setup to protect your health and plumbing fixtures.

  • Do you provide plumbing solutions for commercial businesses?

    We offer commercial plumbing services designed for offices, restaurants, and retail spaces. Our team handles everything from new construction plumbing to routine commercial drain cleaning. We also offer commercial water heaters to keep your daily operations running smoothly.


    When unexpected issues arise, we're equipped to perform prompt commercial plumbing repairs. We know that plumbing downtime can hurt your business, so we work efficiently to resolve problems. If you're looking for seasoned plumbers in Watertown, WI or nearby areas, you can rely on us to maintain your commercial property properly.

  • What kind of drain and pump services do you handle?

    We tackle stubborn clogs using advanced methods like hydro jetting to clear your pipes. Our plumbers also handle routine drain cleaning to prevent future backups from occurring. We work hard to restore proper flow and prevent costly water damage to your property.


    On the pump side, we install and repair both sump pumps and lift stations. A functioning sump pump is essential for protecting your basement from flooding during heavy rains. We'll make sure your drainage systems operate efficiently.

  • Can you help if I need an outdoor water faucet installed?

    We install outdoor faucets and exterior water spigots for residential properties. Adding a new hose bib makes it much easier to handle your outdoor chores. You'll have convenient access to water for gardening, washing vehicles, or cleaning your patio.


    Our plumbers in Watertown, WI ensure these exterior installations are durable and meet all local codes. We route the pipes carefully to provide strong water pressure exactly where you need it. Let us know where you want the spigot, and we'll handle the rest.

  • How do you handle hidden water leaks?

    We offer professional leak detection services to find hidden plumbing issues before they cause massive damage. Water leaks can hide behind walls, under floors, or out in your yard. We use specialized equipment to pinpoint the exact source of the moisture without tearing your house apart.


    Finding the leak early saves you money on water bills and prevents extensive structural rot. Once we locate the compromised pipe, we develop a straightforward plan to repair it. If you're dealing with a hidden leak and need experienced plumbers in Watertown, WI or surrounding areas, count on us to fix it quickly and help you protect your home's integrity.

  • Do you have experience working with septic systems?

    We provide key services to keep your septic system functioning correctly. Our team handles exterior sewer repairs and main line replacements when major issues occur. We work to restore proper wastewater flow away from your home or business.


    We also specialize in septic pump installations, repairs, and full replacements. A failing septic pump can cause serious backups, so we address these problems quickly. We'll evaluate your system and recommend the best approach for reliable performance.

  • Do you use cameras to inspect sewer lines?

    Yes, we utilize sewer video inspections as part of our specialized plumbing services. This technology allows us to see exactly what is happening inside your underground pipes. We feed a specialized camera down the line to locate blockages, cracks, or tree root intrusions.


    Using a camera takes the guesswork out of sewer line repairs and saves you time. Once we identify the exact location of the problem, we can recommend a highly targeted solution. This prevents unnecessary digging and helps us fix your sewer lines much more efficiently.

  • What types of water heater services do you provide?

    We install, repair, and maintain both traditional tank and tankless water heaters. If you want endless hot water, we can upgrade your home to a modern tankless system. We also handle standard tank replacements if your current unit reaches the end of its lifespan.


    When your existing water heater breaks down, we're ready to perform fast repairs. Our technicians diagnose heating issues and replace faulty components to restore your hot water supply.
