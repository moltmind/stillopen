Straight answers

The questions handymen actually ask.

Does it quote small jobs?

It quotes your published hourly rate and minimum, and for list jobs it collects the whole list first so you show up with the right parts.

What about work I don't do?

It only offers what your site says you do. Somebody asks for a full roof tear-off, it says that's not your lane and takes a message.

I'm one guy with a ladder. Is this overkill?

It's the opposite. A big outfit has an office. You have a ladder and a phone that rings while you're on it. This is the office.

How long does setup take?

You give your website address at checkout, it reads your site, and your front desk knows your business before the welcome email lands. Then one line of code goes on your site. On WordPress it's a plugin and there's no code at all. Most people are live in under ten minutes.

What if it says something wrong?

Tell it what to fix from your dashboard and rescan your site. It only knows what your site says, so most wrong answers are a page on your site that's out of date.
